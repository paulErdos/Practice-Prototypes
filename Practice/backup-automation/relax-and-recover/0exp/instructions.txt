Note: setup, and create-backup-archive are all utilities. 

Steps:
1. Install.

2. Run create.sh on the laptop with a destination on the laptop's own
   drive (e.g. the desktop path). This produces the ISO and the archive.

3. Put the ISO on a USB stick, plug in the blank SSD, and boot the
   laptop from the stick.

4. In the rescue environment, run list-drives.sh to identify the
   laptop drive and the SSD.

5. Run mount-drive.sh /dev/<laptop-partition> so the archive is
   reachable.

6. Run recover-to-disk.sh, and when ReaR asks for the disk mapping,
   map the original disk to the SSD.
